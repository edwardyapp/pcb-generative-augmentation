Generation review — can these models draw a defect?

Every image is a 256×256 PNG at 256×256, image-rendering:pixelated, no downsampling. Nothing is cherry-picked: section 2 is random.Random(0).sample over the pool; section 3 shows every sample the epoch-320 conditioning check drew (12/class, all of them). = the VOC bounding box.

0 · Is a defect actually there?

The 6-way classifier cannot answer this — it has no no-defect class, so it labels a blank green board spur with full confidence. That is exactly how the epoch-80 check produced a 22.4% "consistency" from images containing no defect. So we trained a defect / no-defect detector at the scale it is used, with negatives cut from the same boards that provably miss every bounding box, and validated on the held-out boards (94.3% window accuracy). Scores are logit margins, not softmax — softmax saturates at 1.000 on every image and carries no information. The threshold is calibrated to a 5% false-positive rate on provably-clean real board.

The control that makes this fair: generated images are VQ-VAE decodes — soft, smoothed, quantised. So we also score VQ-VAE reconstructions of REAL 600px crops: same decoder blur, but a real defect inside. That is the ceiling a generator must be judged against, and any shortfall below it cannot be blamed on decoder blur.

Read this carefully. A real defect passed through the VQ-VAE decoder is only detectable 14.2% of the time, against 50.0% for the raw photograph. The decoder itself destroys most of the defect at this scale. The generated pools score at or above that ceiling — so at 600px scale we cannot claim the original pipeline fails to draw defects. The information is gone before generation is even involved. That is the crop-scale finding again, arriving from a completely different direction.

And memorisation: mean cosine similarity to the nearest training crop — conditional 0.879, unconditional 0.988 (max 0.992 / 0.995). Every generated crop below is shown beside its nearest neighbour in the set that generator was actually trained on, so you can judge copying with your own eyes.

2b · Your demo_pool — the SAME pipeline, and it agrees

generate_pool.py loads the same three checkpoints (vqvae_560 + pixelsnail_top_357 + pixelsnail_bottom_best), the same unconditional sample_model, the same temp=1.0, the same latent shapes (top 32×32, bottom 64×64). It decodes to 256×256 and then only LANCZOS-upscales to 512 for display — verified: downscaling those 512s back to 256 and re-upscaling changes them by 0.39/255, i.e. the 512 carries no real detail.

So demo_pool and my samples are the same distribution, and the detector agrees: demo_pool 20.0% vs mine 25.0% — 5.0 pp apart. My sampler is not broken, and there is no temperature / checkpoint / resolution / decoding discrepancy to find. 300 images, all 512×512, generated 2026-07-02.
